We won the cup, and our coach said he was proud of us. Watching football is fun, but playing it is much better. In the future, I hope to play for a famous club and win many medals.

In addition, it is a good idea to eat breakfast every morning to get energy for the day. We shouldn't skip meals, and we shouldn't gobble down our food quickly. If we follow these habits, we will feel healthier and more active. Remember: you are what you eat!

Mock Exam 3 (covers Units 7–12)

I. Guided Composition (8 marks) — Model Answer

Laughter is one of the best medicines for the mind and the body. First, laughing improves your mood and lowers your stress levels. It also offers some of the same benefits as exercise because it strengthens the muscles in your face and body. People who laugh often tend to have lower blood pressure. Moreover, laughter helps patients recover from illness more quickly. The harder you laugh, the better you feel. So try to laugh every day — it is free, easy, and very healthy!

II. Free Composition (8 marks) — Model Answer

If I had a million riyals, my life would change in many ways. First of all, I would give some of the money to charity to help poor families. If I helped others, I would feel really happy and satisfied. Then I would buy a beautiful, large, modern house for my family in the suburbs. I would also pay for my parents to go on Hajj, because that has always been their dream. After that, I would invest part of the money in a small business. If the business succeeded, I would create jobs for young people in my city. I might also travel and see the world. Money cannot buy happiness, but if we used it wisely, we could do a lot of good.

I. Guided Composition (8 marks) — Model Answer

I always try to follow healthy eating habits. I never skip breakfast because it gives me energy for the whole day. My favorite dish is kabsa, which my mother cooks every Friday. The aroma of the spices fills the whole house, and the rice is always succulent and delicious. I eat plenty of fruits and vegetables, and I have cut down on fast food and soft drinks. I also drink a lot of water every day. Eating well keeps me fit, healthy, and ready to study.

II. Free Composition (8 marks) — Model Answer

The Olympic Games are the greatest sporting event in the world. They are held every four years, and athletes from all over the globe take part in them. There are summer games and winter games, with sports such as athletics, swimming, football, and skiing. Winning an Olympic medal is the highest honor any athlete can dream of. Athletes train for years, and the harder they train, the better their chances become. The Olympics also teach important values such as respect, friendship, and fair play. Watching the opening ceremony is thrilling because each country marches behind its flag. I hope that one day Saudi Arabia will host the Olympic Games and that our athletes will win many gold medals.

Mock Exam 5 (covers Units 7–12)

I. Guided Composition (8 marks) — Model Answer

Animals can be real heroes, and my neighbor's dog Rex is a perfect example. One night, Rex started barking wildly and would not stop. My neighbor woke up and discovered that smoke was filling the house. Thanks to Rex, the whole family escaped before the fire spread. Animals are said to have a sixth sense for danger, and Rex proved it that night. He stayed alert and refused to leave the house until everyone was safe. The family says Rex should be honored as a hero, and I completely agree. Such loyal and intelligent animals deserve our love and care.

II. Free Composition (8 marks) — Model Answer

If I found a lost wallet full of money, I would do the right thing without hesitation. First, I would look inside the wallet for an ID card or a phone number. If I found the owner's name, I would try to contact him directly. If I couldn't find any information, I would take the wallet to the nearest police station. I would never keep the money, because it simply isn't mine. If I kept it, I would feel guilty every time I thought about it. Honesty is a value that my parents taught me since I was a child. Besides, if I lost my own wallet, I would hope that an honest person would return it to me. Doing the right thing always brings peace of mind.
